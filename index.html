Understanding Autism and Meltdowns

Introduction

We are Year 2 students from the Design for Social Futures (DSF) programme at LASALLE College of the Arts. To support the mobility experiences of autistic and neurodivergent individuals, we have designed the Calm Cabin - a space intended to make public transportation more accessible and comfortable for everyone.

This website explores what it means to have a meltdown, something that people with autism experience as well as a guide on how to use emotion cards. It is designed to support sensory needs and help make public transportation more comfortable for people with ASD, as well as to spread awareness.

Definition of Autism

In Singapore, autism is defined as a neurodevelopmental condition that affects communication, social interaction and behaviour, with support needs varying across individuals. It can present differently from person to person, with each individual requiring different levels of support in daily living, communication or learning.

Reference:
SG Enable. “Disability in Singapore - Types, Statistics, Support | SG Enable,” n.d. https://www.sgenable.sg/about-us/our-impact/disability-in-singapore.

About the Calm Cabin

Feature: Sliding Door

Calm Cabin is designed not only for people with ASD but also for people who need to distance from the noise and overstimulating stimuli in the train. The cabin itself is separated from any other cabins to block the noise and visual elements that might lead people to stress.
